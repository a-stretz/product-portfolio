Overview

I designed and built an AI-assisted career workflow system that moves a candidate from job discovery to application strategy, tailored materials, interview preparation, and answer improvement.

The system is built around a structured candidate knowledge base rather than one-off prompt generation. The core product insight was simple: high-quality career outputs depend on reusable candidate context. A strong story, proof point, positioning angle, or metric should not need to be recreated separately for every application, cover letter, recruiter message, resume version, or interview answer.

The scoped version of the product supports job fit analysis, application decisioning, tailored artifact generation, interview question generation, answer evaluation, rewrite coaching, session review, and workflow history.

01 · Problem

Job search work is fragmented, and generic AI has made it worse.

Candidates manage job descriptions, resumes, cover letters, recruiter messages, company research, interview notes, and story banks across disconnected tools. The result is repeated work and inconsistent positioning across every opportunity pursued.

Generic AI tools can generate drafts, but they lack durable memory of the candidate's experience. They produce polished language, but outputs drift toward repetitive, generic, or weakly grounded claims, exactly the kind of content hiring systems are now trained to filter out.

The practical problem was broader than creating documents. The candidate needed a system that could answer a set of connected questions, not a set of isolated prompts.

The questions the system had to answer
  • Is this role worth pursuing?
  • What parts of my background are most relevant?
  • Which stories and proof points should I use?
  • How should I tailor my positioning for this company or role?
  • What application materials should I generate?
  • What interview questions should I prepare for?
  • How strong are my answers, and how should I improve them?
  • What have I already generated or practiced for this opportunity?

The product needed to create continuity across the entire workflow, not just solve one piece of it.

02 · Product Goal

A structured workflow system, not a collection of generators.

The goal was to build a structured AI workflow system with six capabilities:

  • Evaluate job opportunities against candidate goals and constraints
  • Generate role-specific application materials
  • Maintain a reusable candidate knowledge base
  • Prepare the candidate for role-specific interviews
  • Evaluate and improve interview answers
  • Track outputs, decisions, and progress over time

The system was designed for any professional with objective skill sets, experience with specific products, tools, processes, software, or languages. Because it assists users with diverse backgrounds, it also needed strict guardrails around accuracy: emphasize real experience that demonstrates real capability without under or overstating it.

03 · Product Design

One shared foundation: the candidate knowledge engine.

The product is organized around one shared foundation. Every workflow reads from and writes into the same structured knowledge layer, so context compounds instead of getting recreated.

Knowledge Engine

Stories as reusable product assets.

A story from the candidate's background can support a resume bullet, a cover letter paragraph, an interview answer, a recruiter message, a hiring manager note, or a portfolio summary.

Each story includes context, ownership, actions, outcomes, metrics, tags, strengths, risks, and interview relevance. The richer story vault adds interview questions, STAR structure, debrief notes, and weighting for technical depth, product impact, and transferability.

The system recommends not just "a story" but the right story for the situation.

04 · Core Workflows

Seven connected workflows on one knowledge layer.

Workflow 01 · Intake

Opportunity Intake and Normalization

The system accepts pasted job descriptions, company notes, recruiter context, role labels, source links, or batch imports, and normalizes that input into a structured job record.

CompanyRole title LocationRemote / hybrid / onsite CompensationSeniority Required skillsPreferred skills Product domainDisqualifiers
Output Clean job record for downstream workflows, no more re-parsing raw job text on every run.
Workflow 02 · Fit & Decision

Job Fit Analysis and Decisioning

Evaluates whether an opportunity is worth pursuing. Deterministic checks (location, compensation, hard requirements) are transparent and testable; model reasoning handles nuanced fit, role summarization, and positioning.

  • Responsibility alignment, domain relevance, AI or platform relevance
  • Technical skills match, requirements fit, compensation fit
  • Location, seniority, priority signals, reject criteria
Output Apply / reject / research decision, fit score, priority level, rationale, risk flags, recommended positioning angle, recommended next artifacts.
Workflow 03 · Knowledge Base

Candidate Knowledge Base

The core product layer. Structured information about professional identity, experience, themes, technical-adjacent work, project stories, proof points, positioning, communication weaknesses, writing preferences, and claims that should not be made.

A major design choice: treat stories as reusable product assets with metadata for selection, not prose snippets.

Role in the system Single source of truth consumed by every other workflow — resume, cover letter, interview question, recruiter message, thank-you note, portfolio summary.
Workflow 04 · Artifacts

Tailored Application Materials

Generates application artifacts using the job record and candidate knowledge. The scoped direction replaces variant-based resume recommendation with tailored resume generation from a controlled source of truth.

  • Selects relevant approved bullets
  • Adjusts summary for the target role
  • Emphasizes the right skills and projects
  • Avoids unsupported claims, preserves ownership boundaries
  • Explains what was changed and why
Artifact types Cover letters, recruiter follow-ups, hiring manager notes, LinkedIn connections, fit summaries, thank-you notes, tailored resume versions.
Workflow 05 · Interview Prep

Interview Preparation

Uses the same job context and knowledge base to generate role-specific practice sessions. The user configures style, difficulty, focus area, session length, weakness to test, answer length, and input mode (typed or voice).

Questions are designed to test specificity, decision-making, tradeoffs, product judgment, technical fluency, and business framing. The system also suggests which stories from the knowledge base are most relevant to each question.

Output Focused question set, grounded in both the target role and real background, with recommended stories per question.
Workflow 06 · Answer Coaching

Answer Evaluation and Coaching

Feedback comes in two stages. Editorial markup breaks the answer into spans and labels each section:

  • keep · cut · compress · reorder · upgrade · pivot

Rubric scoring evaluates across relevance, structure, specificity, product judgment, technical fluency, business framing, metrics and outcomes, conciseness, and credibility. The system identifies recurring weaknesses — rambling, missing metrics, weak structure, generic language, no clear result, missing decision logic — and offers rewrite variants (core, full, detailed, more executive, more metrics-driven, more technically accessible).

Loop Answer → review → score → rewrite → retry → compare improvement.
Workflow 07 · History & Memory

History and Workflow Memory

Tracks analyzed jobs, generated artifacts, interview sessions, answer evaluations, recurring weaknesses, and session summaries. The user can return to a role and see what was already analyzed, generated, practiced, and improved. The longer-term direction includes progress tracking across jobs analyzed, roles pursued, applications generated, interviews prepared, recurring answer weaknesses, strongest stories, overused stories, readiness by role type, and improvement over time.

The core product insight was that better AI outputs require a durable candidate knowledge layer. Instead of prompting from scratch for each job, message, resume, or interview answer, the system reuses structured profile facts, stories, proof points, positioning variants, and scoring criteria.
05 · Key Product Decisions

Six decisions that shape how the system behaves.

Decision 01

Knowledge Base First

The strongest design decision. The system does not start from a blank prompt each time. It reuses structured context, so positioning, stories, and metrics compound across workflows.

Decision 02

Human-in-the-Loop Control

The system supports review, editing, overrides, story selection, retries, and rewrite choices. It assists the user without hiding the reasoning behind decisions.

Decision 03

Structured Scoring Over Generic Feedback

The interview system uses dimension-based scoring rather than vague coaching. This makes feedback actionable, comparable over time, and amenable to trend analysis.

Decision 04

Story Selection as a Product Feature

Answer quality depends heavily on story choice. The system helps the candidate match the right story to the right role, question, or artifact — not just produce "an" answer.

Decision 05

Deterministic Rules + Model Reasoning

Hard checks stay deterministic: they must be transparent and testable. Model reasoning handles nuance, drafting, evaluation, story matching, and summarization where judgment is required.

Decision 06

Artifact Memory

Generated outputs are saved, versioned, and tied to the job or session that produced them. This makes the workflow auditable and enables progress tracking across the full job search.

An AI-assisted career workflow system that turns background into reusable structured knowledge, and applies it across the entire job search.

The system evaluates opportunities, recommends pursuit decisions, generates tailored materials, prepares role-specific interview questions, scores answers, identifies recurring weaknesses, and improves responses through rewrite coaching.

This project demonstrates product strategy, workflow design, knowledge architecture, AI system design, prompt orchestration, structured evaluation, and practical AI-enabled product execution.
