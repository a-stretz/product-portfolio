AI Product Case Study

Career Workflow Agent System

Designing a multi-step agentic system to help job seekers evaluate roles, generate positioning, and prepare for interviews.

00

Executive Summary

Built an end-to-end agentic workflow that converts job listings into structured evaluation, tailored positioning, and interview readiness outputs.

01

System Design

Agent 1

Job Parser

Extracts structured requirements, signals, and expectations from raw job descriptions.

Agent 2

Evaluation Engine

Scores roles against candidate fit, priorities, and probability of success.

Agent 3

Positioning Generator

Creates tailored resumes, narratives, and interview responses.

02

Impact

  • Reduced evaluation time per job
  • Improved candidate positioning clarity
  • Increased interview conversion rates
