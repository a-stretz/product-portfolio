Overview

1872 Consulting was an HR-tech consulting firm: IT recruitment, staff augmentation, HR services, and platform modernization for regulated enterprise clients. As a founding-team member, the role spanned product, sales operations, CRM and lifecycle design, and revenue operations at once, closer to a startup co-founder's breadth than a single-function product management seat.

The firm's client-facing operations ran on a legacy AS400 platform. Internally, sales and recruiting ran on manual, fragmented workflows with no repeatable way to convert relationships into pipeline. Growth was capped by process and infrastructure, not by demand from regulated enterprise clients who needed compliance and security guarantees a legacy system made expensive to prove.

01 · Problem

A firm that had outgrown its own systems.

Every part of the business ran on something manual, legacy, or improvised: a client-facing platform old enough to require specialized knowledge to maintain, recruiting relationships that lived in individual heads rather than a shared system, and outbound business development tracked well enough to close deals but not well enough to compound.

  • A legacy AS400 platform serving compliance-sensitive enterprise clients, with no API surface for modern integrations
  • Recruiting and referral relationships that were valuable but not productized into a repeatable offering
  • No internal CRM or lifecycle system, so outbound business development and account management ran on ad hoc tracking
  • Regulated-industry clients who needed provable compliance and security, not just a working product
02 · Approach

Four fronts, one operating core.

Front 01 · Platform Modernization

Retiring the AS400

Owned product discovery and strategy for modernizing the compliance-driven HRIS platform, translating regulatory, security, and enterprise constraints into a scalable system architecture and implementation plan. Led the full product lifecycle to retire the legacy system and launch an integrated, API-enabled replacement.

Outcome 126% revenue growth, including a net-new $1.1M Fortune 100 energy contract.
Front 02 · Automation & Efficiency

API-driven background services

Led product development of fully integrated, API-driven background services for compliance-sensitive client workflows, replacing manual fulfillment steps with automated ones across the client lifecycle.

Outcome 82% faster average fulfillment time; 76% lower labor cost on the same workflows.
Front 03 · Productized Recruiting

Turning relationships into a repeatable offering

Defined product vision, user flows, and MVP scope for an external, referral-based bounty platform, productizing candidate sourcing and network-driven recruiting into a scalable, repeatable service rather than an ad hoc favor economy.

Role 0-to-1 product design: vision, flows, and MVP scope for a customer-facing offering.
Front 04 · CRM & Revenue Operations

The system behind the sales

Designed and implemented the firm's internal CRM and business development platform: outbound workflows, lifecycle states, OKRs, KPIs, and dashboards. Converted manual, legacy, and fragmented workflows into structured processes and operating rhythms across recruiting and business development.

Outcome Operating processes still in use today, years after they were built.
The role was never just product. It was product, sales operations, and revenue operations at once, because at a firm this size, the system you build and the system that sells it are the same system.
03 · Key Decisions

Six decisions across product and revenue.

Decision 01

Modernize Around Compliance, Not Despite It

Treated regulatory, security, and enterprise constraints as the specification for the new architecture, not an obstacle to design around later.

Decision 02

API-First Over Rip-and-Replace

Rebuilt background services as integrated, API-driven automation rather than a monolithic replacement, keeping compliance-sensitive workflows intact while modernizing around them.

Decision 03

Productize the Recruiting Motion

Instead of leaving referral-based sourcing as informal favors, formalized it into a bounty platform with defined vision, flows, and scope.

Decision 04

Build the CRM the Firm Would Keep Using

Designed lifecycle states, OKRs, KPIs, and dashboards meant to become permanent operating infrastructure, not a one-off internal tool.

Decision 05

Early-Adopt Before Competitors Do

Joined the Early Adopter Program for Agency Leads and Clay ahead of the broader market, turning new GTM automation tools into a 58% outbound efficiency gain.

Decision 06

Own the Outcome, Not Just the Roadmap

Paired product ownership with direct account management and revenue operations, including managing a 4-person account executive team against 12 structured KPIs.

Retiring the AS400 platform wasn't an isolated engineering project.
